Questions

Before you load the roll

Is Umber a subscription?

No. Umber is free to download and every film look is included. Umber Pro is a single one-time in-app purchase. No subscription and no ads, at any tier.

Does the look apply to video too?

Yes. The look is rendered live in the viewfinder and recorded with your video, up to 4K at 30 fps in HEVC. Photo mode shoots full 4:3 stills with the same look.

Which iPhones does Umber support?

iPhone 8 or newer, running iOS 15 or later. Umber is not currently available on iPad.

Where are my photos and videos saved?

In the Photos app, in an album called Umber that is created the first time you save something.

Can I use my own LUTs?

Yes. With Umber Pro you can import any .cube file and it sits on the film-box shelf beside the built-in looks.

Does Umber upload my photos or videos?

No. All image processing runs on your iPhone. The only network feature is Describe a Look, which sends just the words you type. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
